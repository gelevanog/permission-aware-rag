# Board Meeting Minutes: September 2026

Fernhill Labs, Inc. — Meeting of the Board of Directors
Date: Thursday 24 September 2026, 15:00–18:30 Lisbon time
Location: Rua da Prata 112, Lisbon, with remote attendance by video

## Attendance

Directors present: Hana Sato (CEO, chair), two investor directors (Series A and Series B leads), one independent director.
Also present: Kofi Mensah (CFO), Grace Liu (General Counsel, secretary), Erin Walsh (VP Engineering, for item 3 only).
A quorum was present. The minutes of the June 2026 meeting were approved without changes.

## 1. Business and financial update

The CEO presented Q3 performance, including the launch of RouteIQ 3.0 on 9 September, 14 new customers in the quarter and an NPS of 52. The CFO presented preliminary Q3 results and the cash position. At the current burn rate, **cash runway is 21 months** as of the end of September. The board asked for a clearer bridge between headcount and revenue growth in the 2027 budget.

## 2. Series C financing

The board discussed the plan to raise a Series C round in the first half of 2027.

- **Target raise: $60 million**, primarily to fund enterprise expansion in North America, platform reliability work and potential strategic acquisitions.
- **Target valuation: $410 million pre-money.**
- The CFO will engage two investment banks for a soft sounding with existing and new investors in November, with a formal process starting in January 2027.
- The investor directors cautioned that customer concentration will be a focus area for new investors.

**Resolved:** management is authorised to prepare the Series C process on the terms above and to engage advisers with fees capped at the amount in the board paper.

## 3. Reliability and the August outage

Erin Walsh presented the postmortem for the 14 August routing outage and the 2027 reliability roadmap, including the migration from Kafka to Redpanda. The board asked for monthly uptime to be added to the board KPI dashboard from Q4 2026.

## 4. Other decisions

- **2026 audit:** the board approved the reappointment of Baird & Lowell LLP as external auditor for FY2026.
- **Option pool:** the board approved an increase of the employee option pool by 2.5% of fully diluted shares, to cover 2027 hiring and refresh grants.
- **Stock options:** the board approved the grants listed in Appendix B (47 grants to new hires and promotions in Q3), at the exercise price from the latest 409A valuation.
- **Litigation:** the General Counsel gave a privileged update on ongoing patent litigation; details are recorded in a separate privileged annex.

## 5. Next meeting

The next regular meeting is on Wednesday 9 December 2026, focused on approval of the 2027 budget. The meeting closed at 18:25.

Signed: Grace Liu, Secretary
