# Project Kingfisher: Proposed Acquisition of Quillstone Analytics

Strictly confidential — leadership team only. Do not forward. Use the codename in all communication, calendar invites and file names.
Authors: Kofi Mensah (CFO) and Hana Sato (CEO). Date: 22 September 2026.

## Proposal

We propose to acquire **Quillstone Analytics**, a 28-person company based in Utrecht, under the codename **Project Kingfisher**, for a total consideration of **$38.5 million**. The proposed structure is 60% cash and 40% Fernhill stock, with $6 million of the cash component held back for 18 months against warranties and a further retention pool for key engineers. Quillstone's two founders would join Fernhill, one as head of the combined forecasting team reporting to Erin Walsh.

## Strategic rationale

Quillstone builds demand forecasting for logistics: it predicts daily order volumes per depot and per postcode up to 14 days ahead, using customer order history, weather and local events. It has around 60 customers, mostly European grocery and parcel companies, with ARR of roughly $4.3 million growing about 55% year on year.

- **Product fit:** forecasting is the natural step before route planning. Combined, we could plan fleets and shifts days in advance, not just on the morning of delivery. This is the most requested capability from our enterprise customers.
- **Customer overlap:** 17 Quillstone customers are already RouteIQ customers; cross-sell potential into our 300+ customer base is significant.
- **Talent:** Quillstone's team includes 9 experienced ML engineers, a profile we have struggled to hire in Lisbon.
- **Competitive:** Routelytics is known to have approached Quillstone in 2025. Owning this capability would differentiate us in enterprise deals.

## Valuation

The price represents roughly 9x current ARR, which is in line with recent transactions for high-growth vertical AI companies, but above our own implied multiple. We expect revenue synergies to justify the premium within three years. A downside case without synergies is included in the financial model (Kingfisher_model_v4 in the leadership drive).
