# Leadership Offsite Notes: Sintra, September 2026

Dates: 16–17 September 2026. Venue: Quinta da Serra retreat, Sintra.
Attendees: Hana Sato, Kofi Mensah, Erin Walsh, Carol Diaz, Grace Liu, Julia Romero (day 2 only).
Facilitator: Hana. Notes: Carol Diaz.

## Day 1: Where we are

We started with an honest look at 2026. What went well: RouteIQ 3.0 shipped on 9 September, ahead of the original plan, the enterprise pipeline is the strongest we have had, and gross margin is improving. What did not go well: the August outage hurt trust with several large customers, hiring in Lisbon is slower than planned, and small-fleet churn on the Starter tier is still too high.

Each leader shared their "one thing that keeps me up at night":
- Hana: dependence on a handful of very large customers.
- Kofi: burn and the timing of the next financing.
- Erin: on-call fatigue and the state of the streaming platform.
- Carol: manager quality in teams that doubled in size.
- Grace: litigation exposure and the enterprise contract backlog.

## Pricing decision

After reviewing win/loss data, discount levels and willingness-to-pay research from 40 customer interviews, the team agreed:

- **Decision: raise the Growth tier price to $85 per seat per month from March 2027** for new customers. Existing customers move at their first renewal after 1 March 2027, with a maximum increase of 8% in that renewal.
- Starter stays unchanged to protect the low end against FleetMind.
- Enterprise remains custom, but the Carbon Reporting module will be packaged as a paid add-on for new Enterprise deals.
- Owner: Julia Romero with Kofi Mensah. Customer communication plan due 30 November. Do not discuss with customers or outside leadership until the plan is approved.

## Day 2: 2027 priorities

We converged on three company priorities for 2027: enterprise expansion, a credible path to profitability, and platform reliability. Hana will turn these into an all-staff memo. We also agreed that each department will cut at least one recurring meeting and one tool by Q1 2027.
