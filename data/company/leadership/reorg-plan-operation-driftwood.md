# Operation Driftwood: Support Team Consolidation Plan

Restricted distribution until the announcement date: leadership team and Head of People, excluding Engineering leadership. Use the codename Operation Driftwood in all communications.
Prepared by: Carol Diaz, Head of People. Date: 28 September 2026.

## Summary

Operation Driftwood consolidates customer support into a single team based in Lisbon. The Austin support team will be merged into the Lisbon team, with North American coverage provided through a follow-the-sun schedule (Lisbon early and late shifts plus two remaining US-based senior engineers working remotely). **6 roles will be reduced**, and the change becomes **effective on 15 January 2027**.

## Rationale

- Ticket volume from North American customers is 31% of total, but the Austin support team represents 45% of support payroll.
- The Lisbon team has better SLA performance and lower cost per ticket, and already handles 40% of North American tickets outside Austin working hours.
- A single team simplifies tooling, training and on-call, and supports the profitability priority for 2027.
- Expected savings are included in the 2027 budget draft as a reduction in Customer Success and Support costs.

## Scope

| Change | Roles |
|--------|-------|
| Austin Support Engineer roles eliminated | 5 |
| Austin Support Team Lead role eliminated | 1 |
| Senior US support engineers retained, remote | 2 |
| New roles opened in Lisbon (late shift) | 3 |

Selection for the retained roles will be based on documented criteria (skills, certification on enterprise accounts, performance ratings from the last two cycles), reviewed by Legal to avoid adverse impact.

## People considerations

- All affected employees receive at least 60 days' notice, satisfying the WARN Act threshold even though it does not formally apply at this size.
- Severance: 8 weeks of base salary plus 1 week per year of service, COBRA premiums paid for 3 months, and outplacement support.
- Affected employees may apply for the new Lisbon roles, with relocation support.
- Support engineering currently reports into the VP Engineering; the merged team will report to the Lisbon Support Lead, who will report to the CEO from 15 January.
