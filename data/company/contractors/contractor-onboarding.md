# Contractor Onboarding Guide

This guide is for independent contractors working with Fernhill Labs, and for the employees who manage them. It complements your statement of work, which always takes precedence on commercial terms.

## Before you start

- **NDA:** you must sign the Fernhill mutual NDA and the contractor agreement before receiving any access. Both are sent through DocuSign by the People team.
- **Deel:** contractors are engaged and paid through Deel. You will receive an invitation to create a Deel profile and upload your tax documents (W-9 or W-8BEN for US-related engagements, NIF and activity registration for Portugal).
- **Your manager at Fernhill** is named in your statement of work. They are your first point of contact for priorities, access and questions.

## Access: what you get

- A Fernhill Okta account with a `-ext` suffix, a YubiKey and a 1Password account.
- Slack (contractor channels plus the channels your manager adds), Google Workspace, Notion (Engineering space), Linear and GitHub (repositories relevant to your work).
- Staging and development environments, with synthetic or anonymised data.
- Selected Engineering documents, such as the architecture overview, API guidelines, deployment process and engineering onboarding.

## Access: what you do not get

- **No access to customer production data**, production databases or production consoles, under any circumstances. If a task seems to require it, your manager will pair you with an employee who has production access.
- No access to HR, Finance, Sales or Legal systems (HiBob, NetSuite, Salesforce, contract repositories).
- No on-call duties and no admin rights in Okta, AWS or GitHub organisation settings.

## Invoicing and timesheets

- Log your hours in Deel. **Timesheets are due by the 3rd business day of each month** for the previous month.
- Invoices are generated **monthly** in Deel from approved timesheets. Your manager approves within 2 business days, and payment follows Deel's standard schedule.
- Hours above any weekly cap in your statement of work are not paid unless your manager approved them in writing in advance.

## Equipment

Contractors normally use their own computer, which must have full-disk encryption, a screen lock of 5 minutes or less and an up-to-date operating system; IT may ask you to install the Kandji agent. A Fernhill laptop can be issued for engagements of six months or longer. All equipment and data are returned or deleted at the end of the engagement, and your access is removed on your last day.
