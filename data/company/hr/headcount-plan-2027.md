# Headcount Plan 2027

Owner: Carol Diaz (Head of People) with Kofi Mensah (CFO)
Version: 0.9, prepared for the leadership meeting on 20 October 2026
Classification: Confidential — leadership and People team.

## Summary

The draft plan adds **22 new roles in 2027**, taking Fernhill Labs from roughly 140 to about 160 people by year end after expected attrition. The plan is weighted towards Engineering and enterprise go-to-market, in line with the CEO's three priorities for 2027. Hiring is phased by quarter so that payroll growth stays below forecast revenue growth.

Phasing: 9 roles in Q1, 7 in Q2, 4 in Q3, 2 in Q4.

## Key requisition: Head of Data

Requisition **HC-2027-114** is for a **Head of Data**, reporting to the VP Engineering, based in Lisbon. The role will own the data platform, the analytics behind Fleet Insights and Carbon Reporting, and our internal BI. It is the most senior new hire in the plan and is expected to open in January 2027 with a retained search firm. Interview panel: Erin Walsh, Hana Sato, Kofi Mensah and one senior engineer.

## Hiring freeze for non-engineering roles

To protect runway while the 2027 budget is finalised, there will be a **hiring freeze on all non-engineering roles from 1 November 2026**. Details:

- Offers already accepted before 1 November are honoured.
- Backfills for regretted leavers in customer-facing roles can be requested as exceptions; each needs CFO and CEO approval.
- The freeze will be reviewed once the board approves the 2027 budget, expected in December.
- Engineering roles already open continue as planned.
