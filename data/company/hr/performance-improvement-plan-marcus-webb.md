# Performance Improvement Plan: Marcus Webb

Employee: Marcus Webb, Support Engineer (Austin)
Manager: Erin Walsh, VP Engineering (support engineering reports into Engineering)
HR partner: Carol Diaz, Head of People
Plan period: 60 days, 1 October 2026 – 29 November 2026
Classification: Confidential — employee, manager and People team only.

## Background

Marcus joined Fernhill Labs in May 2024 as a Support Engineer handling Tier 2 tickets for North American customers. His technical knowledge of the RouteIQ API and telematics integrations is strong and is valued by the team. Since April 2026, however, there have been sustained concerns in three areas, which were discussed in one-to-ones on 12 June, 24 July and 4 September and in the October 2025 and mid-2026 reviews:

- Ticket handling time: median time to resolution for his Tier 2 tickets was 31 hours in Q3, against a team median of 14 hours.
- SLA compliance: 9 of his tickets breached the Enterprise first-response SLA in Q3; the rest of the team combined had 4.
- Documentation: escalations to Engineering frequently lacked reproduction steps, causing rework.

The purpose of this plan is to give clear expectations and support so that Marcus can succeed in the role.

## Goals

By the end of the plan period, Marcus is expected to:

1. Bring his median time to resolution for Tier 2 tickets to **18 hours or less**, measured in Zendesk over the final 30 days of the plan.
2. Have **zero** Enterprise first-response SLA breaches that are attributable to him.
3. Use the escalation template for **every** Engineering escalation, including environment, customer ID, reproduction steps and logs. Erin will review a sample of 10 escalations.
4. Attend the shared support standup at 09:00 Central every working day, or post an async update if absent.

## Support provided

- Weekly pairing session with Rui Carvalho (Support Lead, Lisbon) on ticket triage.
- Access to the Zendesk macros training module, with 4 hours of protected time.
- Reduced queue load (80% of standard allocation) during the first 3 weeks.
