# Manager Guide: Performance Reviews

For people managers at Fernhill Labs. This guide covers the annual review cycle, the rating scale and how to write useful feedback. Questions go to Carol Diaz or #managers.

## The cycle

The annual performance review runs in **October**. The 2026 timeline:

| Date | Step |
|------|------|
| 1 October | Cycle opens in HiBob; self-reviews and peer requests |
| 16 October | Self-reviews and peer feedback due |
| 26 October | Manager reviews and draft ratings due |
| 28 October – 6 November | Calibration sessions by department |
| 9 – 20 November | Review conversations with employees |

Ratings from this cycle feed into the March salary review. People who joined after 1 July 2026 receive a review without a rating.

## Rating scale

| Rating | Name | What it means |
|--------|------|---------------|
| 5 | Exceptional | Consistently far beyond the expectations of the level; a role model others learn from. Expect around 5–10% of people. |
| 4 | Exceeds | Regularly goes beyond expectations in important areas. |
| 3 | Strong | Fully meets the expectations of the level. This is a good rating, and most people should receive it. |
| 2 | Developing | Meets some expectations but has clear gaps; needs a development plan. |
| 1 | Not meeting | Significant gaps; usually already in a formal improvement process. |

## Calibration

Calibration sessions bring together the managers of a department, the department head and a People partner. The goal is consistency: a "4" in Sales should mean the same level of performance as a "4" in Engineering. Come prepared with two or three concrete examples per person. In the session, challenge ratings that rely on recency, likeability or visibility. Distribution guidance exists, but it is not a forced curve. Final ratings are locked in HiBob by the People team after calibration; do not share draft ratings with employees before then.

## Writing feedback

- Be specific. "Led the migration of the ingest service to the new cluster with zero downtime" is better than "great technical work".
- Use the situation–behaviour–impact structure for both praise and criticism.
- Tie feedback to the expectations in the career ladder for the person's level.
- Include at least one forward-looking development goal.
- Avoid comments about personality, age, family situation, health or anything unrelated to work.
- Assume the employee will read every word, because they will.

## The conversation

Book 45 minutes, in person where possible. Share the written review 24 hours in advance so the conversation can focus on the future rather than the rating. Document any disagreement in HiBob. Pay changes are not discussed in this conversation; they come in March.
