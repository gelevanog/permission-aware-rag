# Compensation Review 2026: Working Document

Prepared by: Carol Diaz, Head of People
Reviewed with: Hana Sato (CEO), Kofi Mensah (CFO)
Status: Final for the April 2026 cycle; retained for reference ahead of the March 2027 cycle.
Classification: Strictly confidential — People team, CEO and CFO only.

## Merit budget

Leadership approved an overall **merit budget of 3.8%** of total base payroll for the 2026 cycle, plus a separate promotion pool of 1.2%. The merit budget was set slightly above the Pave market movement for our peer group (3.4% in the US, 3.1% in Portugal) because of elevated engineering attrition in late 2025. Increases were effective on 1 April 2026 and paid in the April payroll.

Allocation guidance given to managers:

| Rating | Guideline increase |
|--------|-------------------|
| 5 — Exceptional | 6–8% |
| 4 — Exceeds | 4–6% |
| 3 — Strong | 2.5–4% |
| 2 — Developing | 0–1.5% |
| 1 — Not meeting | 0% |

## Process

1. Managers received their team's ratings from the October 2025 cycle and band positions in HiBob on 23 February.
2. Proposals were submitted in HiBob by 9 March.
3. People and Finance reviewed for band compliance, equity across gender and location, and budget fit (10–13 March).
4. CEO and CFO final approval on 17 March; managers communicated outcomes 23–27 March.

Anyone below the minimum of their band after the review was brought to the minimum regardless of rating. Seven people received such adjustments.

- Lisbon engineering salaries moved faster than expected; refresh the Portugal bands before March 2027.
