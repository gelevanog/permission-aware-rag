## How to use this log

Each employee relations matter gets a case number in the format ER-YYYY-NNN when it is first raised, whether it comes from a manager, an individual, or the EthicsPoint hotline. This log tracks status and ownership only. Keep entries factual and brief. Update the status within 2 working days of any change. Closed cases are retained for 7 years in line with the HR document retention policy.

Status values: Intake, Investigating, Findings issued, Actions in progress, Closed.
