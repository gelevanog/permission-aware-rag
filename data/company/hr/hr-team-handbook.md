# HR Team Handbook

Internal operating guide for the People team. Owner: Carol Diaz. If a process changes, update this page in the same week.

## Our systems

- **HiBob** is our HRIS and the single source of truth for employee records, time off, org charts, reviews and compensation history. Only People team members have admin rights; managers see their direct reports.
- **Greenhouse** for recruiting and offers. Approved offers sync to HiBob on acceptance.
- **Checkr** for background checks in the US; in Portugal we only request a criminal record certificate (registo criminal) for finance and IT admin roles.
- **Deel** for contractors and for the two employees we employ through an employer of record in Spain.
- **DocuSign** for contracts, offer letters and policy acknowledgements.

## Background checks

Background checks are run only after an offer has been accepted, and the offer is conditional on the result. In Checkr we use the "Standard" package (identity, criminal history for 7 years, employment verification for the last two roles). Finance and IT admin roles also get a credit check where legally allowed. If a check returns something adverse, do not act on it alone: speak to Carol and to Grace Liu in Legal, and follow the pre-adverse action process.

## Onboarding checklist

Owner: People coordinator, with the hiring manager.

1. **Offer accepted:** create the HiBob profile, start the Checkr check, send the welcome email with the start-date logistics.
2. **10 working days before start:** ask IT (via the Okta onboarding workflow) for laptop, YubiKey and accounts; confirm the buddy with the hiring manager.
3. **5 days before:** send the first-week agenda; confirm Kisi badge activation and desk.
4. **Day 1:** welcome session at 10:00, policy acknowledgements in DocuSign, benefits enrolment, office tour, lunch with the team.
5. **Week 2:** check that payroll details and benefits are complete.
6. **6 weeks, 3 months, 5 months:** probation check-in reminders to the manager.

## Offboarding

When someone resigns or is terminated, log the exit in HiBob the same day, notify IT so access can be removed within their 4-hour target, arrange equipment return and schedule the exit interview.

## Document retention

Personnel files, payroll records and employee relations case files are retained for **7 years** after employment ends, then securely deleted. Unsuccessful candidate data is deleted after 12 months unless the candidate agreed to be kept on file. Medical information is kept separately from the personnel file. Never store HR documents in personal Google Drive folders.
