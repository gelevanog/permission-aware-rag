# Offer Letter: Frank Moreau

Fernhill Labs, Inc.
Austin, Texas

14 January 2026

Dear Frank,

On behalf of Fernhill Labs, I am pleased to offer you the position of **Account Executive** in our Sales team, based in our Austin office on South Congress Avenue. You will report to Julia Romero, Sales Manager. We were impressed by your experience selling to logistics and 3PL companies, and we think you will make a real difference to our North American growth.

## Start date and status

Your start date will be **Monday 2 February 2026**. This is a full-time, exempt position. The first 6 months are a probation period, as described in the Employee Handbook. Your employment is at will, which means either you or Fernhill may end it at any time, with or without cause or notice.

## Compensation

- **Base salary:** $84,000 per year, paid semi-monthly.
- **On-target earnings (OTE):** $168,000 per year at 100% of quota, consisting of your base salary plus variable commission of the same amount at target.
- Commission is governed by the Sales Compensation Plan for the relevant year, which you will receive and sign separately. For 2026, a ramped quota applies during your first five months.
- **Equity:** subject to board approval, an option to purchase 6,000 shares of Fernhill Labs, Inc. common stock, vesting over four years with a one-year cliff, under the company's equity incentive plan.

Your salary will be reviewed in the annual March review cycle, starting March 2027.

## Benefits

You will be eligible for Fernhill's standard US benefits from your first day, including medical, dental and vision coverage through Cigna, the 401(k) plan with company match, 25 days of paid time off plus public holidays, the annual learning budget and the home-office stipend. Details are in the Benefits Overview.

## Conditions

This offer is conditional on:

1. satisfactory completion of a background check through Checkr;
2. proof of your eligibility to work in the United States (Form I-9) on your first day;
3. signing our Employee Confidentiality and Invention Assignment Agreement.

## Acceptance

Please confirm your acceptance by signing this letter in DocuSign by **21 January 2026**. If you have any questions about the offer, contact Julia Romero or Carol Diaz.

We are excited to welcome you to Fernhill Labs.

Sincerely,

Carol Diaz
Head of People, Fernhill Labs

Accepted: Frank Moreau, 16 January 2026
