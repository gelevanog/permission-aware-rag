# Relocation Letter: Alice Chen

Fernhill Labs, Inc.
South Congress Avenue, Austin, Texas

Dear Alice,

We are delighted to confirm the arrangements for your move from our Austin office to our Lisbon office. This letter sets out the terms we discussed with you on 22 September. Please read it carefully, and contact Carol Diaz if anything is unclear.

## Your new role and location

You will continue in your role as Financial Analyst in the Finance team, reporting to Kofi Mensah. Your place of work will be our Lisbon office at Rua da Prata 112. You are expected to **relocate to Lisbon by 1 February 2027**, and your first working day in the Lisbon office will be Monday 1 February 2027. From that date, your employment will transfer to Fernhill Labs, Lda, the Portuguese entity, under a Portuguese employment contract that we will send separately. Your continuous service date (March 2023) will be recognised.

## Relocation allowance

Fernhill will pay you a **relocation allowance of €12,400**, paid in two instalments: half with your final US payroll in January 2027 and half with your first Portuguese payroll in February 2027. The allowance is intended to cover shipping of personal belongings, flights for you and your partner, and temporary accommodation. It will be treated as taxable income where required, and you are responsible for any personal tax due. We do not require receipts.

In addition, Fernhill will cover directly:

- immigration support through our provider, including your residence permit application and appointments with AIMA;
- up to two hours of tax advice in each country on the cross-border move;
- 30 days in a serviced apartment in Lisbon if your long-term rental is not ready on arrival.

## Repayment

If you resign within 12 months of your start date in Lisbon, you will be asked to repay the allowance on a pro-rata basis (one twelfth for each month remaining). This does not apply if your employment ends for any other reason.

## Next steps

1. Sign and return this letter through DocuSign by 14 October 2026.
2. Book a call with the immigration provider within two weeks of signing.
3. Agree your handover plan for US-specific responsibilities with Kofi by 15 December 2026.

We are very happy that you will be joining the Lisbon team, and we will do everything we can to make the move smooth.
