# Competitor Battlecard: Routelytics and FleetMind

Owner: Product Marketing with Sales. Updated September 2026 after the RouteIQ 3.0 launch. Shared with all employees so everyone can talk about the market accurately. Never disparage competitors in public or in writing to customers; stick to facts.

## Routelytics

**Who they are:** US-based, founded 2014, roughly 400 employees. Strong with large North American parcel and retail fleets.

**Strengths**
- Large brand and a long list of logos; often the incumbent in big US accounts.
- Mature TMS integrations and a big partner ecosystem.
- Good driver mobile app with proof-of-delivery features.

**Weaknesses**
- Optimisation runs are slow on large plans; customers report 5–10 minutes for 2,000 stops.
- Multi-depot planning is an add-on with separate pricing.
- No native emissions reporting; customers export data to third-party tools.
- EU data residency only on their top tier, and implementation projects frequently take 3–6 months.

**How to win:** run a live bake-off on the prospect's own data and show solve time. Lead with time-to-value (live in weeks) and Carbon Reporting for any customer with EU sustainability obligations.

## FleetMind

**Who they are:** UK-based, founded 2018, around 150 employees. Telematics-first company that added route planning in 2023.

**Strengths**
- Excellent telematics hardware integrations and driver-behaviour scoring.
- Aggressive pricing, often bundled with hardware.
- Strong presence in UK grocery and food distribution.

**Weaknesses**
- Route optimisation is basic: limited support for time windows, HGV restrictions and live re-planning.
- Analytics are tied to their own hardware; mixed fleets are hard to analyse.
- Smaller API surface, no public versioning policy.

**How to win:** focus on route quality and flexibility. Ask how often dispatchers override the plan by hand. Show live re-planning after dispatch and the open telematics integrations (40+ providers).

## Common objections

- *"You're more expensive."* Compare total cost: fuel and overtime savings typically exceed the subscription within the first two quarters. Use the ROI calculator in Salesforce.
- *"We already have route planning in our TMS."* Offer a two-week side-by-side pilot measuring kilometres per drop.
- *"You're smaller than Routelytics."* Point to 300+ customers in 18 countries, our NPS of 52 and reference customers.

## Win/loss snapshot (last 12 months)

Against Routelytics we win about 41% of competitive deals; against FleetMind about 58%. The most common loss reason is price in the small-fleet segment.
