# Sales Compensation Plan 2026

Plan period: 1 January – 31 December 2026. Approved by Kofi Mensah (CFO) and Hana Sato (CEO) on 12 December 2025. Administered by Finance; plan documents are signed individually by each participant in DocuSign.

## Who is covered

This plan covers Account Executives (AEs) in Lisbon and Austin. SDRs and sales engineers have separate plans with a 70/30 split. The Sales Manager's plan is based on team attainment and is documented in her individual plan letter.

## On-target earnings

AE on-target earnings (OTE) are split **50/50** between base salary and variable commission. Variable pay is earned on new annual contract value (ACV), including new logos and expansions; renewals at flat value earn no commission, while renewal uplift counts as expansion.

- **Annual quota for a fully ramped AE: $720,000** in new ACV, measured quarterly (25% per quarter).
- Ramp for new AEs: 0% of quota in month 1, 33% of a monthly quota in months 2–3, 66% in months 4–5, full quota from month 6.
- The base commission rate is the AE's annual variable target divided by annual quota, applied to every dollar of ACV.

## Accelerators and decelerators

| Attainment (annual, cumulative) | Rate |
|---------------------------------|------|
| 0–50% | 0.8x base rate |
| 50–100% | 1.0x base rate |
| Above 100% | **1.5x base rate** |
| Above 150% | 1.75x base rate, subject to CFO review |

Accelerators are applied to the ACV above the threshold, not retroactively to the whole year. Multi-year prepaid deals earn commission on the first-year ACV plus a 10% kicker on the prepaid amount for years two and three.

## Payment timing and clawbacks

- Commissions are calculated by Finance after month-end close and paid in the following month's payroll.
- A deal counts once it is closed-won in Salesforce with a countersigned order form and a valid purchase order where required.
- If a customer does not pay within 120 days of invoice, or cancels within the first 6 months, commission is clawed back from future payouts.
- Deals with discounts that required CEO approval earn commission at 0.8x.

## Disputes and changes

Questions about a calculation go first to Alice Chen in Finance, then to Kofi Mensah. Fernhill may change territories or quotas mid-year with 30 days' written notice; any change applies only going forward. Participants who leave the company are paid commission on deals closed-won before their last day, subject to the clawback rules above.
