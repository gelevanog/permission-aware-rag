# Customer References Programme

Owner: Customer Marketing, with Customer Success. For AEs and anyone who needs a customer voice in a sales process, case study or event.

## Why we run it

Freight and delivery operators trust other operators more than they trust vendors. A 30-minute call with a dispatcher who uses RouteIQ every morning is often the single most persuasive step in a deal. At the same time, our reference customers are busy, so we protect their time carefully.

## Who our references are

We currently have 34 active reference customers who have agreed to speak with prospects, across:

- Parcel and courier (11), grocery and food distribution (8), building materials (6), 3PL (5) and others (4).
- Regions: Iberia (12), rest of Europe (10), North America (12).
- Fleet sizes from 25 to over 1,000 vehicles.

Each reference has a profile in Salesforce (Account > Reference tab) listing the modules they use, the topics they are happy to discuss, preferred language and their limit on calls per quarter. Most references accept at most **2 calls per quarter**.

## How to request a reference call

1. Make sure the opportunity is at **stage 3 (Evaluation) or later** and over 50 seats. We do not use references for early-stage curiosity.
2. Submit a "Reference request" in Salesforce from the opportunity. Include the prospect's industry, fleet size, the specific questions they want answered and three possible time slots.
3. Customer Marketing matches a reference within 3 business days and asks the reference's CSM for approval before reaching out.
4. Brief the reference beforehand (5 minutes is enough) and join the start of the call to make introductions, then leave if the customer prefers to talk privately.
5. Log the call outcome in Salesforce within 2 days and send a thank-you note.

## Thanking references

References receive a thank-you gift after each call (within the $75 gift limit in the Code of Conduct), priority access to beta features and an invitation to the annual customer advisory board in Lisbon. We never offer discounts or credits in exchange for references.
