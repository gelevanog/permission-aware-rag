# Sales Playbook

Owner: Julia Romero, Sales Manager. For Account Executives, SDRs and sales engineers in Lisbon and Austin. Version 2026.3.

## Pipeline stages

All opportunities live in Salesforce and must sit in one of these stages:

| Stage | Name | Exit criteria |
|-------|------|---------------|
| 0 | Prospect | SDR has booked a discovery call with a named contact |
| 1 | Discovery | Pain, current tooling and fleet size confirmed; MEDDICC started |
| 2 | Solution fit | Demo delivered; champion identified; rough scope agreed |
| 3 | Evaluation | Trial or pilot running with success criteria in writing |
| 4 | Proposal | Pricing sent; economic buyer has seen it |
| 5 | Negotiation | Legal and procurement engaged; redlines in progress |
| 6 | Closed won / lost | Signed order form, or a recorded loss reason |

Opportunities with no activity for 21 days are flagged in the weekly pipeline review.

## Qualification with MEDDICC

We qualify every deal over 20 seats using MEDDICC, recorded in the Salesforce MEDDICC fields:

- **Metrics:** the measurable outcome, e.g. "reduce kilometres driven per drop by 8%".
- **Economic buyer:** who signs. Usually the Head of Operations or COO.
- **Decision criteria** and **decision process:** how they will choose and the steps to signature.
- **Identify pain:** the cost of doing nothing — overtime, missed delivery windows, fuel.
- **Champion:** someone inside who sells for us when we are not in the room.
- **Competition:** usually Routelytics, FleetMind or spreadsheets plus a TMS add-on.

## Demo flow

Keep the standard demo to 35 minutes and tailor it with the prospect's own depot addresses where possible.

1. Two-minute recap of their pain in their words.
2. Route Planner: import 300 orders, optimise across two depots, show time windows and HGV restrictions.
3. Live re-planning: add an urgent order after dispatch.
4. Fleet Insights: cost per drop and on-time performance.
5. Carbon Reporting: emissions per customer, if sustainability came up in discovery.
6. Agree next steps and a date before the call ends.

## Pricing and discounts

Always start from list price (Starter, Growth, Enterprise). Multi-year terms, prepayment and seat volume are the legitimate reasons to discount; never discount just to close by month end. Every discount must be requested through the Salesforce approval flow.

## Floor prices and approval thresholds

Internal only. Do not share with prospects or put in emails.

- Growth tier: the absolute floor price of $31 per seat per month, for 3-year prepaid deals above 500 seats only. No deal may go below this under any circumstances.
- Discount up to 10% off list: AE discretion.
- 10–20%: Sales Manager (Julia Romero) approval.
- 20–35%: CFO approval.
- Above 35%: CEO and CFO approval, with a written business case.
