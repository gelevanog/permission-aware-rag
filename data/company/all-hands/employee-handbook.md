# Employee Handbook

Welcome to Fernhill Labs. This handbook covers the day-to-day basics of working here. It applies to all employees in Lisbon, Austin and those working remotely, and it is maintained by the People team. If something here conflicts with your local employment contract, your contract wins. Questions go to Carol Diaz, Head of People, or to #ask-people in Slack.

## Working hours

We do not track hours, but we do care about overlap. Everyone is expected to be available during core hours, 10:00–16:00 in their local time zone, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can arrange your day however works best for you and your team. Meetings that span Lisbon and Austin should be scheduled in the Lisbon afternoon (which is the Austin morning) and kept under 50 minutes where possible.

If you regularly need to be offline during core hours, for example for school pickups, agree a pattern with your manager and set it in your Google Calendar working hours so colleagues can see it.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off per year, plus the public holidays of the office location in their contract. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated amount. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the first quarter of the following year; anything beyond that expires on 31 March.

We actively encourage people to take at least one block of 10 consecutive working days off each year.

## Requesting time off in HiBob

All time off is requested in HiBob (Time Off > Request). Choose the policy type (Vacation, Sick, Parental, Bereavement or Other), add the dates and a short note if useful. Your manager gets a notification and should respond within 3 working days. Requests longer than 5 days should be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance. Sick days do not need pre-approval, but please log them in HiBob on the day you return.

## Probation

New employees have a 6-month probation period. Your manager will hold a check-in at roughly 6 weeks, 3 months and 5 months. Either side may end employment during probation with the shorter notice period stated in your contract.

## Salary reviews

Salary reviews happen once a year in March, with any changes taking effect on 1 April. Promotions outside the cycle are possible but rare. See the Compensation Philosophy document for how we think about pay.
