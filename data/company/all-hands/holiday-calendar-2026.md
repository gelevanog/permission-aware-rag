# Holiday Calendar: Late 2026

This calendar lists the remaining public holidays for 2026 in our two offices, plus company-wide days off. Remote employees follow the public holidays of the country in their employment contract. HiBob already has these dates loaded, so you do not need to request them as time off.

## Company-wide days

- **Fernhill Day — Friday 18 September 2026.** Our annual company day off, marking the anniversary of the first RouteIQ customer going live. The whole company is offline; on-call coverage is handled by volunteers who receive a day in lieu.
- **Winter shutdown — Thursday 24 December 2026 to Friday 1 January 2027.** The company closes for the holidays. Shutdown days do not come out of your 25 days of paid time off. On-call and a small support rota stay active; anyone on the rota gets the equivalent days back in January.
