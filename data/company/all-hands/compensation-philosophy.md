# Compensation Philosophy

Owner: Carol Diaz, Head of People. Approved by the leadership team in January 2026.

This document explains how Fernhill Labs decides what to pay people and why. It deliberately contains no figures. We want everyone to understand the system, even though individual pay remains private.

## What we are aiming for

We aim to pay **at the 60th percentile of the market** for each role, level and location. That means we pay a bit better than the median company of our size and stage, without trying to compete with the largest public tech companies on cash. We benchmark against salary survey data for venture-backed SaaS companies in Portugal and the US, using Pave as our main data source.

We want pay to be fair, explainable and consistent: two people doing the same role at the same level in the same location should be paid in the same range, and differences within the range should come from experience and performance, not negotiation skill.

## Salary bands

Every role is mapped to a level and a location, and each combination has a salary band. Bands are reviewed annually by People and Finance against fresh market data and approved by the CEO and CFO. Individual salaries and salary band figures are confidential and only available to HR and people managers. If you want to understand where you sit in your band, ask your manager — they can discuss your position in general terms.

## Annual review cycle

- Performance reviews run in October.
- Salary reviews happen in **March**, using the October review outcomes, with changes effective on **1 April**.
- The size of the overall merit budget is set by leadership each year based on company performance and market movement.
- Promotions normally take effect with the April cycle; exceptional mid-year promotions need CEO approval.

## Equity

All permanent employees receive stock options when they join. We also make **equity refresh grants** to strong performers and to people who are promoted, typically awarded with the April cycle and vesting over four years with no cliff. Your grant details are visible in Carta.

## Transparency

We share the philosophy, the process and the levels openly. We do not publish individual salaries or band figures. You are always free to discuss your own pay with anyone you like; we will never penalise anyone for doing so.
