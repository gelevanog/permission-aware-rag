# Code of Conduct

Approved by the CEO and General Counsel, March 2026. Applies to every employee, contractor and board member of Fernhill Labs.

Our customers trust RouteIQ with data about their fleets, drivers and deliveries. That trust depends on how we behave with each other, with customers and with the wider world. This code is short on purpose; it describes the standard, not every situation.

## Respect at work

We treat each other with dignity regardless of role, nationality, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, religion or background. Harassment, bullying and discrimination are not tolerated, whether in the office, on Slack, at an offsite or at a customer's site. Disagree about ideas as hard as you like; never attack the person.

Lisbon and Austin have different working cultures and languages. Default to English in shared channels, be patient with people working in a second language, and assume good intent.

## Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest exists when a personal interest could influence, or appear to influence, your judgement at work. Examples include holding a stake in a vendor or competitor, hiring a relative, or doing paid side work for a logistics company. Having a conflict is not wrong in itself; hiding it is. Disclose potential conflicts to your manager and to Legal (Grace Liu) using the disclosure form in Notion. Legal keeps a register and will tell you if any steps are needed.

## Gifts and hospitality

You may accept or give gifts with a value of up to $75 (or the local equivalent) per person per year from any single company. Anything above that must be declined or reported to Legal. Never accept cash, gift cards or anything offered during an active procurement or contract negotiation. Reasonable business meals are fine and are covered by the Expense Policy.

## Speaking up

If you see something that seems wrong, you have several ways to raise it:

- your manager, or your manager's manager;
- Carol Diaz (Head of People) or Grace Liu (General Counsel);
- the **EthicsPoint hotline**, available 24/7 by web or phone, where you can report anonymously.

Reports are handled confidentially and investigated by someone independent of the issue.

## Non-retaliation

Fernhill Labs will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or takes part in an investigation, even if the concern turns out to be unfounded. Retaliation is itself a serious breach of this code and can lead to dismissal.
