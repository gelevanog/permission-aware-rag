# Travel Policy

Owner: Finance (Kofi Mensah, CFO). Last reviewed: July 2026.

Fernhill Labs people travel to visit customers, attend conferences and spend time with colleagues in the other office. This policy keeps travel safe, comfortable and sensibly priced. When in doubt, spend company money the way you would spend your own.

## Booking

All flights, hotels and trains must be booked through Navan, using your Fernhill account. Navan applies our policy limits automatically and gives Finance a single view of where people are, which matters if there is ever an emergency. Bookings made outside Navan will only be reimbursed if Navan genuinely could not offer the option (for example, a regional rail ticket in Portugal) and you explain why in the expense note.

Book at least 14 days ahead for flights wherever possible.

## Approvals

- Domestic travel (within the country of your office) needs no pre-approval beyond a calendar entry, as long as it fits the limits below.
- International travel requires your manager's approval in Navan before booking. Trips between Lisbon and Austin count as international.
- Trips longer than 10 days or with a total estimated cost above the team's quarterly travel budget also need sign-off from the department head.

## Flights and trains

- Flights under 6 hours of scheduled flying time: economy class.
- Flights of 6 hours or more: premium economy is allowed. Business class is not covered unless a medical need has been agreed with People.
- For trips under 4 hours by train (for example Lisbon–Porto), take the train.
- One checked bag is reimbursable on any flight.

## Hotels

The nightly cap is $220 per night in the US and €180 per night in Europe, excluding taxes. In very expensive cities or during major trade shows, Navan may show a higher dynamic cap; that is acceptable. Staying with friends instead of a hotel is fine, but we do not pay a cash alternative.

## Per diem

Instead of itemised meal receipts, travellers receive a daily allowance for meals and incidentals:

| Location | Per diem |
|----------|----------|
| United States | $65 per day |
| Portugal | €55 per day |
| Other countries | Navan country rate |

On the first and last day of a trip, the per diem is 75% of the daily rate. Meals provided by a conference or customer should not also be claimed. Per diem is claimed through Ramp at the end of the trip.

## Safety

Register your trip in Navan, keep your phone charged and save the travel assistance number in your contacts. If anything goes wrong, contact your manager first and Finance second.
