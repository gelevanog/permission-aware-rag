# Product Overview: RouteIQ

RouteIQ is Fernhill Labs' route-planning and logistics analytics platform for freight and delivery companies. This overview is meant for every employee, so you can explain what we sell in a few sentences. For deeper material, see the product docs at docs.routeiq.test and the Sales enablement folder.

## Who it is for

Our customers run fleets of 10 to 5,000 vehicles: parcel carriers, grocery and food distributors, building-materials suppliers, regional freight hauliers and third-party logistics providers. The typical buyer is a Head of Operations or Head of Transport; daily users are dispatchers and fleet managers.

## Modules

**Route Planner.** Builds optimised daily routes across one or many depots, respecting time windows, vehicle capacities, driver shifts, low-emission zones and road restrictions for heavy goods vehicles. Since RouteIQ 3.0, the multi-depot optimiser can rebalance stops between depots and re-plan live when an order is added after vehicles have left.

**Fleet Insights.** Dashboards and alerts on utilisation, on-time performance, idle time, cost per drop and driver behaviour, using telematics data from more than 40 integrated providers (including Samsara, Webfleet and Geotab).

**Carbon Reporting.** Calculates emissions per route, per customer and per shipment following the GLEC Framework and ISO 14083. Customers use it for their own sustainability reporting and to give their clients per-delivery emissions figures.

**API.** A REST API (currently v2) for pushing orders in and pulling routes, ETAs and analytics out. Most mid-market and enterprise customers integrate RouteIQ with their TMS or ERP through the API.
