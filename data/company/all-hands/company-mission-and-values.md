# Company Mission and Values

## Our mission

Every day, millions of parcels, pallets and containers move on routes that waste time, fuel and people's patience. Fernhill Labs exists to **make every mile count**. We build RouteIQ so that freight and delivery companies of any size can plan better routes, understand their fleets and cut their emissions, without needing an in-house data science team.

## Our values

**1. Start from the dispatcher's desk.**
Our real users are dispatchers, fleet managers and drivers working under pressure at 5 a.m. We design for them first, visit them often, and judge features by whether they make a hard morning easier.

**2. Make it measurable.**
We are an analytics company, so we hold ourselves to the same standard. Goals have numbers, experiments have hypotheses, and we say "we don't know yet" when we don't.

**3. Own the outcome.**
If you notice something broken, it is yours until you have handed it to someone who agrees to own it. We celebrate people who fix the boring thing nobody asked them to fix.

**4. Candour with care.**
We tell each other the truth early, including bad news, and we do it kindly. Feedback goes to the person first, not to their manager or a Slack thread.

**5. One team, two cities.**
Lisbon and Austin are one company. We rotate meeting times fairly, write things down so nobody depends on being in the right room, and visit each other's office at least once a year.

## A short history

- **2019:** Fernhill Labs is founded in Lisbon by Hana Sato and two co-founders who had spent years building routing software for a European parcel carrier. The first office is a shared space in Cais do Sodré.
- **2020:** RouteIQ 1.0 launches with 12 customers in Portugal and Spain, just as the pandemic makes home delivery explode.
- **2022:** Series A. The Austin office opens to serve North American customers.
- **2024:** Series B. The Carbon Reporting module launches.
- **2026:** RouteIQ 3.0 ships in September. The company has around 140 employees and serves more than 300 customers in 18 countries.

## How we use the values

The values show up in hiring scorecards, in performance reviews and in the questions leadership asks before big decisions. If you think we are falling short of one of them, say so — that is value number four.
