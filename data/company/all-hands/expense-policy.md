# Expense Policy

This policy explains what Fernhill Labs reimburses and how to claim it. It works alongside the Travel Policy, which covers flights, hotels and per diems. Finance owns this document; questions go to #finance-help in Slack.

## How to submit

All expenses are submitted in Ramp. If you have a Ramp card, upload the receipt in the Ramp app or forward it to receipts@fernhill.test straight after the purchase. If you paid personally, create a reimbursement request in Ramp with the receipt attached.

- Receipts are required for any expense above $25 (or the euro equivalent).
- Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date. Claims older than 90 days will not be reimbursed.
- Every expense needs a short business purpose, e.g. "Lunch with Transportes Silva ops team, renewal discussion".
- Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run after your manager approves.

## Home-office setup

Every employee who works from home at least part of the week can claim:

- A **one-time home-office stipend of $500** (or €460 in Portugal) for a desk, chair, monitor or similar. Laptops and core peripherals come from IT and are not part of the stipend.
- An **internet allowance of $40 per month** (or €37), paid automatically through payroll. You do not need to submit receipts for this.

## Client entertainment

Meals and events with customers or prospects are encouraged when they help the relationship. Keep it reasonable. Any client entertainment expected to cost more than $150 in total needs pre-approval from your manager before the event, logged as a pre-approval request in Ramp. List all attendees and their companies on the claim. Alcohol is fine in moderation as part of a meal; a bar tab on its own is not.

## Not reimbursable

- Personal travel extensions, upgrades and loyalty fees
- Traffic or parking fines
- Gym memberships (use the wellness allowance instead)
- Gifts to colleagues, unless part of an approved team budget
- Alcohol without a meal, minibar charges, in-room movies
- Lost or stolen personal items
- Childcare or pet care while travelling

## Misuse

Deliberately claiming personal costs as business expenses is treated as a conduct issue under the Code of Conduct. Honest mistakes happen; if you spot one, tell Finance and we will reverse it without fuss.
