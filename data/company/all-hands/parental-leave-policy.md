# Parental Leave Policy

Effective 1 January 2025, updated June 2026. Applies to all permanent employees in Portugal and the United States from their first day of employment, regardless of gender or how they become a parent (birth, adoption, surrogacy or foster placement).

## How much leave

| Caregiver type | Leave | Pay |
|----------------|-------|-----|
| Primary caregiver | 16 weeks | 100% of base salary |
| Secondary caregiver | 6 weeks | 100% of base salary |

The leave can be taken any time within the first 12 months after the child arrives. Primary caregiver leave must be taken in one continuous block; secondary caregiver leave can be split into up to three blocks of at least one week each.

Where statutory leave is more generous (for example the Portuguese *licença parental*), the statutory entitlement applies and Fernhill tops up pay to 100% where social security pays less. In Portugal, the People team handles the Segurança Social paperwork with you.

## Notifying HR

1. Tell Carol Diaz or another member of the People team as early as you feel comfortable, and at least **12 weeks** before your expected leave start date. Everything you share is confidential until you decide to tell your team.
2. Agree a start date and an outline handover plan with your manager.
3. Submit the leave request in HiBob under "Parental leave" once the dates are confirmed.
4. For adoptions or surrogacy, notice periods are flexible — we know dates can change at short notice.

## While you are away

Your benefits, including health insurance and pension contributions, continue as normal. You will not be asked to work, and your manager will not contact you except for agreed keep-in-touch days (up to 10, paid, entirely optional). Performance reviews take your leave into account; you will never receive a lower rating because you were on leave.

## Phased return

After primary caregiver leave, you can choose a **phased return**: up to 4 weeks at 60% of your normal hours on full pay. Agree the pattern with your manager before your leave ends. Lactation rooms are available on floor 3 in Lisbon and in the Austin wellness room.
