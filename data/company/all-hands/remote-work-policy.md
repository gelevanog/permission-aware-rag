# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Effective 1 February 2026. Applies to all employees attached to the Lisbon or Austin office. Fully remote employees are covered by their individual contracts, but the work-from-anywhere and equipment sections apply to them as well.

## The hybrid model

Fernhill Labs is a hybrid company. People attached to an office are expected to work from it **2 days per week**. Tuesday and Thursday are our anchor days: most team rituals, planning sessions and the Thursday lunch happen on those days, so please make them your office days unless your manager has agreed something different.

The other three days are flexible. Teams can add an extra in-person day for sprint planning, offsites or onboarding a new colleague; managers should give at least one week's notice.

We do not use attendance tracking software. Kisi badge data is only used for security and fire safety, never for performance purposes.

## Work from anywhere

You may work from a location other than your home or office for **up to 4 weeks per year** in total. The weeks do not need to be consecutive. Conditions:

- Tell your manager at least 2 weeks in advance and log it in HiBob under "Work from anywhere".
- You must still be available during core hours of your home office's time zone.
- For locations outside the EU or US, check with People first. Some countries create tax or immigration issues even for short stays.
- You need a reliable connection; working from a phone hotspot for a full week is not realistic.

## Equipment

IT provides a laptop, a monitor, a keyboard and a mouse for your home setup. Additional furniture can be bought with the one-time home-office stipend described in the Expense Policy. Equipment remains company property and must be returned when you leave.

## Ergonomics and wellbeing

A good setup prevents long-term problems. Please make sure that:

- the top of your screen is at or slightly below eye level;
- your chair supports your lower back and your feet rest flat;
- you take a short break away from the screen at least every hour.

People can arrange a free remote ergonomic assessment with our provider; request it through #ask-people. If you notice that a colleague seems isolated working remotely, mention it to them or to their manager — connection matters as much as productivity.
