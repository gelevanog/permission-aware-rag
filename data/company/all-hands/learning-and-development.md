# Learning and Development

We want everyone at Fernhill Labs to be noticeably better at their job a year from now than they are today. This page explains the support available. The programme is run by the People team; send ideas and questions to #learning.

## Learning budget

Every employee has a **learning budget of $1,500 per year** (calendar year, not rolling). Contractors are not eligible. The budget can be used for:

- online courses and subscriptions (e.g. O'Reilly, Coursera, egghead);
- books, physical or digital;
- certifications and exam fees (AWS, Kubernetes, CPA modules, Salesforce admin);
- conference tickets and workshops, including related travel;
- language classes — Portuguese for Austin colleagues who relocate is especially popular.

Spend under $300 needs no approval: buy it, and submit it in Ramp with the category "Learning". Anything larger needs a quick approval from your manager in Ramp first. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.

## Conference attendance

- Each team can send people to conferences within its own budget; the cost of the ticket comes out of the individual's learning budget, and travel comes out of the team travel budget.
- If you are **speaking** at a conference, the full cost (ticket and travel) is covered by the company and does not touch your learning budget. Tell Marketing early — they will help with slides and promotion.
- After a conference, share a short write-up or a 10-minute summary at a tech talk so the knowledge spreads.

## Mentoring programme

Twice a year (February and September) we match mentors and mentees across teams and offices. Pairs meet for one hour every two weeks for six months. Mentees set two or three goals at the start, and the People team checks in at the halfway point. Sign-up forms are posted in #learning; current cohort runs until March 2027.

## Internal tech talks

Tech talks happen **every other Friday at 15:00 Lisbon time** (09:00 in Austin), in the 4th-floor event space and on Zoom. Talks are 20–30 minutes plus questions, and anyone can give one — recent topics include vehicle routing heuristics, Postgres query plans, negotiating renewals and how carbon factors are calculated. Recordings are kept in the Learning section of Notion.

## Career conversations

Learning plans are discussed in the October review cycle and revisited mid-year. Engineers can also use the Engineering Career Ladder to plan their growth.
