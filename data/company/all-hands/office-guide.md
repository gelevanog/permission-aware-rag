# Office Guide: Lisbon and Austin

Everything you need to know to find your way around our two offices. Facilities questions go to #office-lisbon or #office-austin in Slack.

## Lisbon

**Address:** Rua da Prata 112, 1100-417 Lisboa, floors 3 and 4. The entrance is next to the pastelaria; take the lift on the left.

- **Floor 3:** People, Finance, Sales, Legal, the leadership area and four quiet rooms ("Alfama", "Belém", "Graça", "Estrela").
- **Floor 4:** Engineering, Product and Design, plus the event space used for all-hands (seats about 120).

The office is open 07:30–20:00 on weekdays. The nearest metro stations are Baixa-Chiado (green and blue lines) and Terreiro do Paço (blue line).

## Austin

**Address:** South Congress Avenue, suite 400, Austin, Texas — a five-minute walk from the river. The Austin office is one floor with 45 desks, two meeting rooms ("Barton" and "Pedernales") and a phone booth corridor. Open 07:00–19:00 on weekdays.

## Access and badges

Both offices use **Kisi** for door access. Your Kisi credential is activated on your first day; you can use the mobile app or the physical badge. Visitors must be registered in Kisi by their host at least one hour ahead, and they receive a temporary link. Lost badges should be reported to IT (it@fernhill.test) immediately so they can be revoked.

## Wi-Fi

- Employees: connect to **Fernhill-Corp** using your Okta credentials (certificate-based, set up automatically on managed laptops).
- Guests: connect to **Fernhill-Guest** (Lisbon) or **Fernhill-Guest-ATX** (Austin). The guest password rotates monthly and is displayed on the screen at reception.

Never connect personal or test devices to Fernhill-Corp.

## Parking and getting there

Lisbon has no employee parking; the closest public garage is at Praça da Figueira, and we reimburse parking only for customer visits. There are bike racks in the courtyard. Austin has 12 reserved spaces in the building garage, allocated first come, first served each morning, plus validated visitor parking on request.

## Lunch

On anchor days (Tuesday and Thursday) we provide lunch in both offices. Lisbon uses a rotating set of local caterers; Austin orders from a different restaurant each week. Dietary requirements can be set in the #lunch form. On other days the kitchens are stocked with fruit, coffee and snacks.
