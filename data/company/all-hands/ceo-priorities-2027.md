# CEO Memo: Our Priorities for 2027

From: Hana Sato, CEO
To: All Fernhill Labs staff
Date: 5 October 2026

Team,

2026 has been the year RouteIQ grew up. We shipped 3.0, crossed 300 customers and opened a new floor in Lisbon. I am proud of what we built, and I am also clear-eyed about what got harder: larger customers expect more from us, our infrastructure was stretched by the August outage, and investors now reward efficient growth rather than growth at any cost. With that in mind, the leadership team has agreed three company priorities for 2027. Every team plan and every OKR should trace back to at least one of them.

## Priority 1: Enterprise expansion

Our fastest-growing segment is fleets of 500+ vehicles, and those customers buy differently. In 2027 we will make RouteIQ the obvious choice for them. That means completing SOC 2 Type II, shipping granular role-based permissions and audit logs, offering a contractual uptime SLA, and building a small enterprise sales and customer success motion out of Austin and Lisbon. Success looks like enterprise accounts making up a clearly larger share of new business by the end of the year.

## Priority 2: A credible path to profitability

We will keep investing, but every investment needs a clear return. Concretely: we will hold headcount growth below revenue growth, keep improving gross margin through infrastructure efficiency, and review every recurring vendor contract. Kofi will share department budget targets in November, and he and I will be transparent about how we are tracking each quarter at all-hands.

## Priority 3: Platform reliability

The 14 August routing outage reminded us that dispatchers cannot plan their day if RouteIQ is down at 6 a.m. Reliability is a feature. In 2027, Engineering will dedicate meaningful capacity to the tech-debt roadmap, improve our failover between regions and tighten our incident process. Our target is 99.95% monthly uptime for the core planning service, every month.

## What I am asking of you

- Read your team's draft 2027 plan when your manager shares it, and challenge anything that does not connect to these priorities.
- Say no to work that does not serve them, politely and with reasons.
- Keep raising questions in the monthly AMA; the next one is on 5 November.

Thank you for an extraordinary year. Let's make 2027 the year we earn the trust of the biggest fleets in Europe and North America.

Hana
