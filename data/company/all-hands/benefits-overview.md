# Benefits Overview 2026

This page summarises the benefits Fernhill Labs offers in the US and Portugal. Full plan documents are in HiBob under Documents > Benefits. Benefits enrolment for 2027 opens on 2 November 2026.

## Health insurance

- **United States:** medical, dental and vision through Cigna. Fernhill pays 90% of the employee premium and 70% for dependants. You can choose between the Open Access Plus PPO and a high-deductible plan with an HSA; on the HSA plan Fernhill contributes $1,000 per year.
- **Portugal:** private health insurance through Médis, covering the employee and spouse or partner and children at no cost to the employee. Dental is included with a reimbursement limit per year.

Coverage starts on your first day of employment.

## Retirement

US employees can join the 401(k) plan administered by Guideline. Fernhill matches 100% of your contributions up to 4% of base salary, and the match vests immediately. In Portugal, Fernhill contributes to a PPR (Plano Poupança Reforma) savings scheme at 3% of base salary after probation.

## Learning, leave and wellness

- **Learning budget:** $1,500 per person per year for courses, books, certifications and conferences. See Learning and Development.
- **Parental leave:** up to 16 weeks fully paid for primary caregivers and 6 weeks for secondary caregivers. See the Parental Leave Policy.
- **Wellness allowance:** $60 per month (or €55) for gym, sports clubs, therapy apps or similar, claimed through Ramp.
- **Employee assistance programme:** free, confidential counselling sessions via Modern Health, up to 8 sessions per year.

## Other perks

- Lunch on anchor days in both offices
- Two weeks of work from anywhere per year beyond the standard allowance for employees with 5+ years of tenure
- Bike-to-work scheme in Lisbon and commuter benefits in Austin

## Executive benefits

Members of the executive team (C-level and VPs) receive the following in addition to the standard package:

- An executive car allowance of $1,150 per month, paid through payroll (or the euro equivalent for Portugal-based executives).
- An annual executive health check at a private clinic, including cardiology screening, booked through the People team.
- Supplemental life insurance at 4x base salary, on top of the standard group policy.

These benefits are reviewed by the board's compensation committee each year.
