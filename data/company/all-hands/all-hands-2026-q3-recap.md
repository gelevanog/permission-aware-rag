# All-Hands Recap: Q3 2026

All-hands held on Thursday 1 October 2026, Lisbon 4th floor and Austin main room, streamed on Zoom. Presenters: Hana Sato (CEO), Erin Walsh (VP Engineering), Julia Romero (Sales Manager), Carol Diaz (Head of People). Recording is in the #all-hands channel for 90 days.

## Q3 highlights

- **RouteIQ 3.0 launched on 9 September.** The release brought the new multi-depot optimiser, live ETA sharing for end customers and a redesigned Fleet Insights dashboard. Hana thanked the route-planner team, who shipped two weeks earlier than the original plan.
- **14 new customers** signed in the quarter, including our first two customers in Poland. We now serve customers in 18 countries.
- **NPS reached 52**, up from 46 in Q2. The biggest improvement came from mid-market customers who adopted the new onboarding flow.
- Support median first response time fell to 41 minutes.

## People and places

- **11 new hires** joined in Q3, seven of them in Engineering. Welcome to all of them; introductions are in #intros.
- The **Lisbon office now has a new floor**: we took over the 4th floor at Rua da Prata, which houses Engineering and a large event space. The 3rd floor has been re-planned with more quiet rooms.
- Carol reminded everyone that the performance review cycle opens in October and that benefits enrolment opens on 2 November.

## Q4 focus areas

1. Stabilise RouteIQ 3.0: close out the top 20 customer-reported bugs and hit 99.95% monthly uptime.
2. Enterprise readiness: SSO/SCIM for all tiers, audit logs, and the SOC 2 Type II observation window.
3. Close the year strongly in sales, with a particular push on the Growth tier.
4. Plan 2027 — the CEO's priorities memo will follow in October.
