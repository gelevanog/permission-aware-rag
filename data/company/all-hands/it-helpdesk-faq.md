# IT Helpdesk FAQ

The IT team supports employees and contractors in both offices and remotely. Most questions are answered below; for anything else, contact the helpdesk.

## How do I contact IT?

- **Slack:** post in **#it-help**. This is the fastest route for most questions.
- **Email:** **it@fernhill.test** creates a ticket automatically.
- **In person:** the IT desk is on floor 3 in Lisbon. In Austin, IT visits on the first Tuesday of each month.

Helpdesk hours are **08:00–18:00 Lisbon time**, Monday to Friday (that is 02:00–12:00 in Austin). Outside those hours, urgent issues that block customer-facing work can be escalated through the on-call IT contact listed in the #it-help channel topic.

## How do I request software?

Open the "Software request" form in the IT section of Notion, or ask in #it-help. Tell us what you need, why, and whether it will touch customer data. Common tools (Figma, Postman, Docker Desktop, JetBrains IDEs) are usually approved the same day. New tools that store company or customer data need a short security review and may take up to 5 working days. Please do not sign up for SaaS tools with your work email without a request; we try to keep an accurate inventory.

## When will my laptop be replaced?

Laptops are replaced **every 3 years**, or earlier if they fail or cannot run the tools you need. IT will contact you about two months before your replacement date. You hand in the old laptop when you collect the new one; IT wipes and recycles or donates it. Damage should be reported straight away — accidents are covered, and nobody will be charged for an honest mistake.

## I lost my YubiKey or laptop. What now?

Tell IT immediately via #it-help or it@fernhill.test, even outside helpdesk hours. We will revoke the device or key and issue a temporary access method. Speed matters more than the explanation.

## Can I use my personal phone for work?

Yes, for Slack, Google Workspace and Okta Verify, provided the phone has a passcode and an up-to-date operating system. Customer data must never be stored on a personal phone.

## How do I get access to a system I don't have?

Request it through the Okta dashboard ("Request app"). Your manager approves, and for sensitive systems the system owner approves as well.
