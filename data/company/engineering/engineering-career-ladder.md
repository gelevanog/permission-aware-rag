# Engineering Career Ladder

Owner: Erin Walsh, VP Engineering. Version 3, adopted January 2026.

The ladder describes what we expect from engineers at each level. It is used for hiring, for performance reviews in October and for promotion decisions. It is a guide, not a checklist; impact matters more than activity.

## Levels at a glance

| Level | Title | Typical scope |
|-------|-------|---------------|
| L1 | Associate Engineer | Well-defined tasks with close guidance |
| L2 | Software Engineer I | Features within a service, with some guidance |
| L3 | Software Engineer II | Owns features end to end; reliable on-call participant |
| L4 | Senior Engineer | Owns a service or a significant area; leads projects across a team |
| L5 | Staff Engineer | Shapes technical direction across several teams |
| L6 | Senior Staff Engineer | Company-wide technical strategy in a domain |
| L7 | Principal Engineer | Sets long-term architecture for the whole platform |

L4 is a career level: there is no expectation that everyone progresses beyond it. Engineering managers have a parallel track (M1–M4), documented separately.

## Expectations by level

**L1–L2.** Write clear, tested code and ask for help early. Learn the codebase, the deployment process and the on-call basics. By L2, deliver small features with limited supervision and give useful code reviews.

**L3.** Break down a feature into tasks, estimate it and deliver it to production with monitoring. Take a full part in the on-call rotation. Improve the code you touch and mentor interns or L1 engineers.

**L4 (Senior Engineer).** Lead multi-person projects of one to three months from design document to rollout. Make sound trade-offs between speed and quality, and explain them to product managers. Be the go-to person for at least one service (for example `route-planner` or `ingest`). Actively mentor L2–L3 engineers.

**L5 (Staff Engineer).** Identify and solve problems that span teams, such as cross-region failover or the Kafka migration. Write the technical strategy for an area and get buy-in. Grow other senior engineers.

**L6–L7.** Set direction for the platform over a horizon of years and represent Fernhill externally.

## Promotions

Promotions are decided in the October cycle and take effect on 1 April. A manager writes a promotion case with evidence of sustained performance at the next level for at least six months; it is reviewed by a panel of two engineers at or above the target level plus Erin Walsh.

## Salary bands

Base salary bands for US-based engineers (Austin and US remote), 2026. Portugal bands are maintained separately in euros by People.

- Software Engineer II (L3): $129,000 – $153,000
- Senior Engineer (L4): $158,000 – $186,000
- Staff Engineer (L5): $192,000 – $228,000

New hires are normally offered between the minimum and midpoint of the band. Offers above the midpoint require approval from the Head of People.
