# Contract Templates Guide

Owner: Grace Liu, General Counsel. This guide helps you pick the right template and know who can sign. All templates live in the Legal space in Notion and in DocuSign as pre-built envelopes. Always use the latest version; never edit an old signed contract to create a new one.

## Which template to use

| Situation | Template | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| Exploring a partnership, sharing roadmap or pricing under confidentiality | **Mutual NDA** (v4) | Two-year term, covers both parties. Use for prospects, partners and vendors. |
| A new customer buying RouteIQ | **Master Services Agreement (MSA)** plus an **Order Form** | The MSA is signed once; each purchase or renewal is a new Order Form. |
| A customer whose personal data we will process (almost all of them) | **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** | Attached to the MSA. Includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and our sub-processor list. |
| Small customers buying online | Online Terms of Service | No negotiation; accepted at checkout. |
| A vendor or contractor providing services to us | Vendor Services Agreement or Contractor Agreement | Contractors are engaged through Deel using our agreement. |

If a counterparty insists on their own paper, send it to Legal through the #legal-requests form. Expect 5 business days for a first review of a customer MSA redline and 2 business days for an NDA.

## What you can change without Legal

- Company names, addresses, dates and contact details.
- In the Order Form: product, seats, term and prices within approved discounts.
- In the Mutual NDA: the purpose clause.

Anything else, including liability caps, indemnities, governing law, payment terms longer than 45 days and uptime SLA credits, needs Legal review.

## Signature authority

| Signatory | Can sign |
|-----------|----------|
| CEO (Hana Sato) | Any contract |
| CFO (Kofi Mensah) | Any contract; required for all contracts above $500,000 total value alongside the CEO or General Counsel |
| General Counsel (Grace Liu) | NDAs, DPAs and contracts up to $500,000 |
| **VPs** | Contracts up to **$100,000** total contract value within their own budget |
| Managers and individual contributors | Mutual NDAs on the standard template only |

Total contract value means the full value over the whole term, not the annual value. Splitting a contract into smaller pieces to stay below a threshold is not allowed.

## After signing

Countersigned contracts are filed automatically from DocuSign into the contract repository. For customer contracts, make sure the signed Order Form is attached to the closed-won opportunity in Salesforce so Finance can invoice.
