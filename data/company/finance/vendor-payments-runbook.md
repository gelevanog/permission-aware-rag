# Vendor Payments Runbook

Owner: Finance operations. Applies to all outgoing payments to suppliers and contractors not paid through Deel or payroll.

## Payment schedule

Payment runs happen **every Tuesday and Friday**. Bills must be approved in Ramp Bill Pay by 12:00 Lisbon time the working day before the run to be included. Urgent off-cycle payments are possible but need written CFO approval and a reason (for example, a supplier threatening to suspend a critical service).

All USD and EUR payments go out from our **Mercury** operating accounts. EUR payments to Portuguese suppliers use the Mercury EUR account via SEPA; other currencies are converted at payment time.

## Approval rules

- Every bill needs approval from the budget owner of the department that ordered the goods or service.
- **Dual approval** is required for every payment run: one person in Finance prepares the run in Ramp, and a second, different person releases it in Mercury. Nobody may both prepare and release the same run.
- Any single payment **above $10,000** (or the equivalent in other currencies) additionally requires approval from the **CFO**, Kofi Mensah. When he is unavailable, the CEO can approve.
- Payments to a vendor whose bank details changed in the last 30 days need a call-back verification before release (see below).

## Adding a new vendor

1. The requester submits the "New vendor" form in Ramp with the vendor's legal name, address, tax ID (NIF or EIN), contact and the contract or quote.
2. Finance checks the vendor against sanctions lists (via Ramp's built-in screening) and, for anything touching customer data, confirms that IT has completed a security review.
3. Bank details are collected only through the vendor's official invoice or a signed letter on company letterhead, never through email alone.
4. Finance calls the vendor on a phone number from their website or contract (not from the email) to confirm the bank details.
5. The vendor record is activated in Ramp and synced to NetSuite.

## Changing bank details

Bank detail change requests are the most common fraud attempt we see. Treat every one as suspicious. Verify by phone using a number already on file, record who you spoke to in the vendor's Ramp notes, and get a second Finance team member to approve the change.

## After the run

Ramp syncs paid bills to NetSuite automatically. Finance checks the sync on the morning after each run and reconciles the Mercury accounts weekly. Any failed payment is investigated the same day.
