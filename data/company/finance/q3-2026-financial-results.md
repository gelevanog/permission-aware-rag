# Q3 2026 Financial Results (Management Accounts)

Prepared by: Alice Chen, Financial Analyst
Reviewed by: Kofi Mensah, CFO
Period: 1 July – 30 September 2026. Close completed 9 October 2026 (business day 6).
Classification: Confidential — Finance and leadership. Unaudited.

## Headline numbers

| Metric | Q3 2026 | Q2 2026 | Q3 2025 |
|--------|---------|---------|---------|
| Revenue | $14.82 million | $13.9 million | $10.7 million |
| ARR at quarter end | $61.3 million | $57.6 million | $44.2 million |
| Gross margin | 71% | 69% | 68% |
| Net burn (quarter) | $5.7 million | $6.4 million | $7.1 million |
| Cash and equivalents | $40.1 million | $45.8 million | $63.0 million |

Revenue grew 6.6% quarter on quarter and 38% year on year. Subscription revenue was 94% of the total; the rest is implementation and training services.

## ARR movement

- Opening ARR: $57.6 million
- New business: +$2.9 million (14 new customers)
- Expansion: +$1.6 million, mostly Growth-tier customers adding seats and the Carbon Reporting module
- Contraction: −$0.3 million
- Churn: −$0.5 million (6 customers, all on Starter)
- Closing ARR: $61.3 million

Net revenue retention on a trailing 12-month basis was 116%.

## Gross margin

Gross margin improved two points to 71%. The main driver was the AWS Savings Plan signed in June and the move of batch route optimisation jobs to Graviton instances, which lowered hosting costs by about 11% versus Q2. The 14 August routing outage had a one-off impact on revenue through service credits, booked as a reduction in revenue in the quarter.

## Operating expenses and burn

Operating expenses were up 4% versus Q2, mainly from 11 new hires and the fit-out of the new floor in Lisbon (one-off cost of $0.6 million). Net burn fell to $5.7 million, or roughly $1.9 million per month, as collections improved after we moved 40 customers from quarterly to annual upfront billing.

## Cash position and outlook

Cash and equivalents were $40.1 million at 30 September, held at Mercury (operating) and in a treasury money-market fund. At the current burn rate this gives approximately 21 months of runway, before any new financing. The Q4 forecast assumes revenue of $15.6 million and net burn of $5.2 million. Key risks: two large renewals in Q4 and the EUR/USD rate, as about 35% of our costs are in euros.
