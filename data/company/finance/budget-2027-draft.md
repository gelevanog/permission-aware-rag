# Budget 2027: Draft v2

Owner: Kofi Mensah, CFO. Model maintained by Alice Chen.
Status: Draft for leadership review — not approved. Do not share department figures outside Finance and leadership until the board approves the budget.

## Assumptions

- Revenue: $72 million for 2027 (base case), ending ARR of $80 million.
- Headcount: 22 net new roles phased through the year, per the draft headcount plan.
- Merit increases budgeted at 3.5% from 1 April 2027.
- EUR/USD at 1.10 for planning. Each 5-cent move changes total costs by about $0.9 million.
- AWS spend grows at half the rate of revenue thanks to the Redpanda migration and reserved capacity.

Engineering figures cover salaries, benefits, contractors, tooling and engineering travel. The increase funds the reliability and enterprise feature hires and the Head of Data role.

## Marketing reduction

The marketing budget is reduced by 12%. Paid acquisition for the Starter tier showed the weakest payback in 2026 (over 30 months), so spend on paid search and display will be cut, two smaller trade shows dropped, and the remaining budget focused on enterprise events (Manifest in Las Vegas, Transport Logistic in Munich) and customer marketing.

## Open questions

- Whether to keep the Austin office lease after it expires in September 2027 or move to a smaller space.
- Contingency: currently 2% of total opex, held centrally by the CFO.
- Sales commission accrual assumptions depend on the final 2027 compensation plan.
