## Lessons from last year

- Customer contract samples took two weeks to gather because signed copies were scattered across email and DocuSign. All signed contracts are now stored in the Legal contract repository.
- The auditor raised a minor finding on the timeliness of user access removal. IT now targets offboarding within 4 hours of termination and keeps evidence.
- Intercompany balances did not reconcile at year end by a small amount; reconcile monthly from now on.

## During fieldwork

Keep a daily 15-minute stand-up with the audit team at 09:30 Lisbon time. All requests go through Alice so we can track them in the PBC tracker. Do not send documents by email attachment; use the shared folder.
