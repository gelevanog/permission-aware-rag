# Finance Team Handbook

How the Finance team works. Owner: Kofi Mensah, CFO. Team: Kofi Mensah, Alice Chen (Financial Analyst), a senior accountant in Lisbon, and our outsourced payroll and tax advisers.

## Systems

- **NetSuite** is the general ledger and the source of truth for revenue, billing and the subscription ledger. Two subsidiaries: Fernhill Labs, Lda (Portugal) and Fernhill Labs Inc. (Delaware, operating in Texas).
- **Ramp** handles corporate cards, reimbursements and bill pay. It syncs to NetSuite daily.
- **Mercury** for banking.
- **Salesforce** is the source for bookings; closed-won opportunities flow into NetSuite via the billing integration.
- **Google Sheets** for the operating model, maintained by Alice. A move to a planning tool is under evaluation for 2027.

## Month-end close

We aim to **close the books by business day 6** of the following month.

| Business day | Tasks |
|--------------|-------|
| BD1 | Ramp and card transactions coded; payroll journals posted |
| BD2 | Revenue schedules run in NetSuite; deferred revenue roll-forward |
| BD3 | Accruals (contractors, hosting, commissions); bank reconciliations |
| BD4 | Intercompany reconciliation between the Portuguese and US entities |
| BD5 | Flux analysis versus budget and prior month; reviewer sign-off |
| BD6 | Lock the period; management accounts to leadership |

## Revenue recognition basics

We follow ASC 606. In short:

- Subscription fees are recognised **ratably** over the subscription term, starting on the date the customer gets access.
- Implementation and training services are recognised as they are delivered, usually over the first 4–8 weeks.
- Annual upfront invoices are recorded as deferred revenue and released monthly.
- Service credits are treated as a reduction of revenue in the period they are granted.
- Multi-year deals with ramped pricing are recognised on a straight-line basis where the service is the same each year; check with Kofi before booking any non-standard contract.
