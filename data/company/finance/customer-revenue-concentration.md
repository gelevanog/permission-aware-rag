# Customer Revenue Concentration Analysis

Prepared by: Alice Chen, Financial Analyst, for Kofi Mensah (CFO)
Data as of: 30 September 2026 (ARR from the NetSuite subscription ledger, reconciled to Salesforce)
Classification: Confidential — Finance and leadership.

## Why this matters

Investors and our auditors both look at customer concentration. A high dependency on a single customer increases the risk to revenue forecasts and affects valuation in the upcoming financing. This analysis is refreshed every quarter and was requested by the board's audit and finance committee.

## Key findings

- **Northgate Freight accounts for 18.6% of ARR**, making it by far our largest customer. Its contract covers 31 depots across the UK and the Netherlands and renews in June 2027.
- The **top 10 customers represent 47% of ARR**, up from 44% a year ago, driven by enterprise expansions.
- The top 25 customers represent 63% of ARR.
- Outside the top 10, no single customer exceeds 2.5% of ARR.
- 68% of ARR is billed in euros or pounds; 32% in US dollars.

## Concentration by segment

| Segment | Customers | Share of ARR |
|---------|-----------|--------------|
| Enterprise (500+ vehicles) | 19 | 52% |
| Mid-market (50–499 vehicles) | 104 | 35% |
| Small (under 50 vehicles) | 184 | 13% |

## Risks

1. **Renewal risk on the largest account.** Losing or significantly discounting the largest account would reduce ARR materially and break several 2027 plan assumptions. Their procurement team has already signalled a competitive review that includes Routelytics.
2. **Expansion dependency.** About 40% of 2026 expansion ARR came from three customers.
3. **Sector exposure.** Over half of ARR comes from parcel and grocery distribution, which are sensitive to consumer spending.

## Mitigation plan

- Executive sponsor programme: Hana Sato sponsors the top 3 accounts, other leadership members the next 7, with quarterly business reviews.
- Start the renewal of the largest account 9 months early with a multi-year offer including the Carbon Reporting module.
- Grow the mid-market base, where pipeline is healthiest, so concentration falls naturally. Target: top-10 share below 42% by end of 2027.
- Report concentration metrics in the quarterly board pack, and flag any customer above 10% of ARR in the financing data room with a mitigation narrative.
